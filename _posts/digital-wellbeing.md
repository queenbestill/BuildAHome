# Digital wellbeing, one small habit at a time

You don't need to throw your phone away to feel the difference. Small, repeatable habits are usually enough to start getting your attention back. Here are a few to try.

## 1. Keep the first hour of your day screen-free

Before you reach for your phone, give yourself a little time that is only yours: a glass of water, a stretch, a coffee by the window, a few quiet minutes. You choose how your day starts, instead of letting notifications choose it for you.

**A small trick:** charge your phone outside the bedroom and use a simple alarm clock.

## 2. Silence the notifications you don't need

Most alerts are not urgent. Keep only the ones from the people and tools you truly need, and let the rest wait until you decide to look.

## 3. Create phone-free moments

Meals, conversations and walks are good places to start. Leave the phone in another room, or face down and out of reach, and notice how much more present you feel.

## 4. Give your evenings a soft landing

Try to put your screen away a little while before bed. Read, stretch, write, or simply sit in silence for a few minutes.

## 5. Start small, and be kind to yourself

Pick one habit and try it for a week. There is no perfect way to do this. What matters is noticing what helps you and repeating it.
